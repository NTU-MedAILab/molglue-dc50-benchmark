# Data and figure licence boundary

No third-party database-content licence is granted or sublicensed here. Raw
MGTbind, MGDB, MolGlueDB and TPDdb exports and every row-level derivative are
absent from the public payload.

The aggregate evidence tables, protocols, legends and figures were created by
the authors, but their final public content licence is still an author
decision. Until that choice is made, no blanket licence is inferred. Any later
content licence must explicitly exclude third-party database records and local
runtime products.
